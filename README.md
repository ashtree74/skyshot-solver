# Skyshot Solver

Research on blind astrometric calibration and compact stellar-pattern retrieval by Adam Jesionkiewicz.

This initial publication contains two English research manuscripts, dated 11 October 2026.

**Implementation source code is coming soon and will be published in this repository.**

## Research

| Manuscript | PDF |
| --- | --- |
| **Skyshot Solver: Blind astrometry with nested stellar dominance levels and compact local descriptors** — the complete method, its relation to existing approaches, and experimental evaluation. | [Read the paper](research/skyshot-method.pdf) · 13 pages |
| **LGQ-GD: A Learned 32-bit Retrieval Key for Stellar Neighbourhoods** — the learned encoder, quantization, uncertainty-ranked probing and comparisons with earlier retrieval keys. | [Read the paper](research/skyshot-learned-key.pdf) · 11 pages |

The manuscripts document the evaluated revisions, datasets, measurement conditions and limitations. Independent evaluation and discussion are welcome.
